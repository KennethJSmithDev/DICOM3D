Anatomy & imaging, grounded in evidence.

An open-source interactive anatomy and medical imaging platform. Explore what is available, what is being prepared and which questions remain research.

Roadmap review candidate — not deployed. Milestones are proposed boundaries, not promised dates. Alpha.1 is a research/educational release, not a clinically validated diagnostic tool.

Expanded male anatomy and the female composite, contextual learning, Scientific/Holographic/Materialization presentations, and the public H000 CT/SEG example.

Acceptance, evidence & limitations

Acceptance boundary

Source-native selection, picking, isolation, opacity, camera and lifecycle at recorded scopes; hardware WebGPU and separately forced WebGL2; independently qualified static, Core and IPM paths.

Evidence

Limitations

Planned#

Finish justified source-native atlas corrections

Resolve remaining male source-admission cases where publisher evidence permits and improve the female composite through explicit shared, replacement and exclusion mappings.

Acceptance, evidence & limitations

Acceptance boundary

Exact component/compound membership, laterality and unchanged transforms; real selection/isolation; counts by category; remaining holds preserved rather than guessed away.

Evidence

Current atlas scope; BodyParts3D attribution; HRA attribution.

Limitations

Eighteen residual files have overlap/laterality dispositions. A held file is not necessarily a missing organ. No mirroring, relabeling or new geometry solely to inflate a count.

Media status

Planned#

Proposed v0.8 direction — connected imaging and learning

Compose existing ingestion, Orthanc/IRIS retrieval and source-linked imaging into a usable supported-study path, while standalone anatomy remains independent.

Acceptance, evidence & limitations

Acceptance boundary

Authorized retrieval reaches the frontend with verified source identities, values and spatial transforms; clear authentication/failure states, source-linked findings where supported, and beginner contextual learning.

Evidence

Architecture; installation scope; current acceptance.

Limitations

Historical retrieval and an installed IRIS viewer are not a packaged live PACS workflow. DICOM SR/SEG, FHIR findings and generic local admission need explicit contracts and gates. AI Hub is a separate deployment path.

Media status

Use public/synthetic studies for distributable examples; private imaging and reports stay local.

Acceptance, evidence & limitations

Agreed feature scope, stable compatibility contracts, independent installation/use/reproduction, accessible learning/navigation, accepted limitations, maintainable tests and support/rollback instructions.

DICOM3D includes two unmodified source files from UMD.zip (Figshare file
44111183). UMD_221129_001_t2.nii.gz is the acquired sagittal T2-weighted
MRI and UMD_221129_001_seg.nii.gz is its published annotation.

DICOM3D assigns display colours, a grayscale window and a cropped viewing
area around the published labels. Reformatting and voxel-face surfaces
use the spatial transform supplied by the source. This example was
acquired independently of the H001 pregnancy case and the fetal atlas.

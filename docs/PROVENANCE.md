# Provenance and publication boundary

H000 qualification used a public research CT study and existing segmentation. Accepted evidence binds source study, segmentation, reconstruction, semantic ID, spatial transforms, GLB and manifest. Detailed receipts remain in the separate research workspace and CompDocs; this repository is not their archive.

The local research viewer accepts a manifest and matching GLB and verifies the declared SHA-256. Published v0.5.1 distributions do not bundle the H000 pixels or derived lesion. Historical receipts and publication flags remain unchanged.

The v0.6.0 web/source release is **reference-only**. It excludes the five H000 candidate payload files and CT screenshot from public Git/Pages. Local qualification and source licensing do not supply exact project approval to publish acquired data. The earlier CT-inclusive candidate remains preserved separately. See [the public data policy](PUBLIC_RESEARCH_DATA_POLICY.md).

Generalized BodyParts3D/HRA references retain source identity, coordinates, coverage limitations and original notices. They are not registered to each other or to patient anatomy. Presentation changes do not create new source observations, segmentation or clinical evidence.

DICOM3D-owned software remains Apache-2.0 with personal copyright ownership; source anatomy retains distinct licenses. Raw DICOM, private patient data, local paths, private metadata and research receipts are excluded from the public experience. This repository boundary is not an independent medical privacy audit.

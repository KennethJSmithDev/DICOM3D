# Published ultrasound examples

The two images are from Fariha Altaf and Biza Akbar, “Rescue Cervical Cerclage in a Twin Pregnancy With Short Cervical Length or Cervical Dilatation: A Case Report,” *Cureus* 18(7), e113224 (2026). Copyright © 2026 Altaf et al. [Article and original figures](https://doi.org/10.7759/cureus.113224) · [CC BY 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Both figures belong to the same published case. Figure 1 shows two ultrasound views of the twin pregnancy described by the authors as dichorionic diamniotic. Figure 2 shows the fibroid the authors identify in the anterior uterine wall. The HRA teaching specimen is a separate source.

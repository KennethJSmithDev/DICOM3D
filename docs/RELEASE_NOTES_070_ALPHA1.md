# DICOM3D v0.7.0-alpha.1 — Anatomy & Imaging Foundation

A research **prerelease** for exploration and feedback. The versioned GitHub tag identifies the exact release-source commit; its qualified runtime artifacts retain source `623d81bcab9081a0522281be36a698d16ecbda4b`. Source publication, downloadable artifacts and the live-demo deployment are independently identified surfaces.

DICOM3D is an open-source interactive anatomy and medical imaging platform designed to help people see, explore and understand the human body and its underlying medical images.

## Available at the recorded qualification scope

- Three.js/R3F with hardware WebGPU on the RX 7900 XT and separately exercised forced WebGL2 compatibility.
- Expanded BodyParts3D reference: 1,205 male draw owners in 17 independently loadable bundles. The 1,235-owner female composite shares that base, excludes/replaces nine owners and adds 39 HRA owners / 60 source components with a disclosed approximate fit.
- Exact source-component and compound selection, picking, opacity, isolation/restoration and focus. Source components, overlapping concept views, physical spans, aliases and 706 broad proxies remain separately accounted; they are not additive organ counts. Eighteen source files remain held.
- Beginner/Explore/Evidence contextual learning, Scientific and Holographic presentations, and an approximately eight-second Materialization introduction with explicit Skip/reduced-motion controls.
- Public H000: 103 source CT frames, retained stored values/geometry and SEG relationships, source-linked highlighting, an NRX/R002 display-derived lesion surface, and WebGPU direct CT-volume presentation.
- Independently scoped static ZIP, fresh Docker Core and authenticated IPM installation/restart/uninstall evidence. The exact [acceptance matrix](RELEASE_070_ALPHA_ACCEPTANCE.md) and [download hashes](DOWNLOADS_070_ALPHA.md) define the boundaries.

## Important limits

Generalized anatomy is not registered patient anatomy. The female fit and manually positioned comparison are approximate; the shared male-base skeleton/exterior is not a separately reconstructed female body. Whole CT display is direct volume rendering, not whole-scan organ segmentation. The native NRX research renderer remains independent; smoothing can lose thin features and global surface accuracy is unproven.

No native MSI/EXE, arbitrary-study interpretation, clinically validated diagnosis/treatment, packaged live Orthanc/IRIS retrieval, Docker Hub/IPM registry/OEX update, or installed upgrade/settings/private-data rollback is claimed. Earlier startup fetch uncertainty, same-origin worker-cache behavior and the documented IRIS Entry/login-after-restart requirement remain disclosed.

DICOM3D code remains Apache 2.0. BodyParts3D and HRA retain separate CC BY 4.0 attribution and historical notices; public H000 retains its independent source/research terms. No private studies, reports, credentials or qualification directories are release assets.

The v0.6.5 tag, downloads, branding assets and historical evidence remain preserved. The alpha's new screenshots replace README presentation only; the original screenshot files are retained. Issues and public-safe feedback are welcome; sponsorship supports usability, learning, source-backed coverage, reproducible distribution and future interoperability work.

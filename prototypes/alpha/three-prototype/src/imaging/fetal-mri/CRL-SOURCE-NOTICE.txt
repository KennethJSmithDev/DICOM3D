# CRL2025 T2W Atlas and Segmentations

This is a spatio-temporal map of the developing fetal brain, from gestational age (GA) weeks 21 to 37, made from 194 reconstructed fetal T2-weighted brain images. Included are two sets of image segmentations: tissue segmentations with delineated borders between white and cortical gray matter (cortical plate), and regional parcellations which overlap the white-gray matter border. The tissue segmentation is further divided into two versions, one which splits white matter into subplate and intermediate zone (white matter zones; WMZ), and one that does not. WMZ segmentations are only supplied up to 32 weeks gestational age.

File suffixes:
_atlas		- T2w Atlas Image
_region		- Regional segmentation
_tissue		- Tissue segmentation
_tissueWMZ 	- Variant of tissue segmentation with subplate and intermediate zone boundary delineated

T2WAtlas_labelkey-region.txt	- Label key for regional segmentation
T2WAtlas_labelkey-tissue.txt	- Label key for tissue segmentations
Label keys can be loaded in ITK-SNAP

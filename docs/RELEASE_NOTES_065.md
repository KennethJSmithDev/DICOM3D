# Proposed v0.6.5 release notes

This private candidate assembles the Babylon workstation, source-backed male and female reference anatomy, scientific presentation, verified CT admission and the curated public H000 CT/SEG demonstration. Selectable groups represent source ownership mappings, not a claim of complete anatomical coverage. The HRA asset is losslessly distributed as verified chunks.

The public Safe Demo does not require IRIS, Orthanc or AI. It does not support general DICOM import, patient-to-atlas registration, diagnostic interpretation or clinical use. The IRIS retrieval research and native renderer are not distributed integrations. Distribution and publication remain subject to independent approval and the relevant source licenses.
